✦ Written From the Heart ✦

Letters I
Never Sent

Some feelings are too vast for conversation. These are the words that live in the quiet spaces between everything I say to you.

Letter I 🌙

On the Nights I Miss You

There are nights when the city is loud but the silence around me is deafening because you are not in it. I look at the distance between us on a map — 35 kilometers — and I think: the moon is 384,000 kilometers away, and it still pulls the entire ocean toward itself. You pull me like that.

— Always yours, R.
Letter II ⭐

On What You Don't Know

You don't know how often I think of you mid-day, doing something completely ordinary — and suddenly you arrive in my mind, uninvited and utterly welcome. You are the thought that interrupts everything and I have never once wanted it to stop.

— R.
Letter III 🥀

On Your Darkness

You love black roses — and I understand why. You are not afraid of dark things. You find beauty where others see nothing. That is rare. That is you. You taught me that the most extraordinary things exist outside the ordinary.

— R.
Letter IV 💎

On Why It's You

If I could choose anyone in this whole world — in any city, any time — I would walk back through every road, every kilometer of Dhaka, every confused turn, just to find you again. You were worth every moment of not knowing you.

— Always, R.
Letter V 🌹

On the Distance

Savar to Tongi. I have measured it on maps, I have traced the roads with my eyes. But Sinthi — the real distance between us has always been zero. Because wherever you are is exactly where my heart already lives.

— Yours completely, R.
Letter VI 🕯️

On Forever

They say forever is a long time. But with you, it doesn't feel long enough. I want a thousand forevers. I want to run out of time with you and still be reaching for more. You are the only thing that makes time feel both infinite and fleeting.

— Forever yours, Rezaul.
