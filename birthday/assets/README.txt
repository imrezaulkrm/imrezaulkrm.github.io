Put your media files here, matching the filenames referenced in js/config.js:

  waiting.mp3   - soft ambient/waiting music
  birthday.mp3  - birthday background music
  voice.mp3     - a short voice-over clip, e.g. "Hey... Happy birthday."
  photo1.jpg ... photoN.jpg - photos for the gallery scene

The site works fine even if some (or all) of these are missing - it just
skips them gracefully.
