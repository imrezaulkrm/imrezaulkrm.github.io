About Me

Hello! I'm Omar Faruk

I was born on 13th March 2002 in Savar, Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I spent most of my life. Growing up in a rapidly developing urban environment allowed me to witness technological progress firsthand, which gradually shaped my interest in digital systems and interactive media.

Academically, I have always been dedicated and disciplined. I completed my SSC in 2017 with a GPA of 5.00 and my HSC in 2019 with a GPA of 3.83. Beyond academics, I am a focused, determined, and reflective individual. I am approachable and capable of working effectively within a team, especially as a sports enthusiast (cricket, football, and badminton).

"Game development is the perfect blend of creativity, logic, and storytelling. It's where art meets technology."

My inspiration comes from my father's hard work, discipline, and perseverance, as well as the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (SAW), which guide my personal integrity and commitment.

My curiosity about how games function, how mechanics are programmed, and how user interaction is structured transformed into a clear ambition: to create my own games. Game development appeals to me because it combines creativity with logic, storytelling with systems design, and imagination with technical execution.
